# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Theatre Vision welcomes people who want to improve the project. We provide a
respectful environment for everyone, regardless of experience, nationality,
identity, or background.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Give clear and constructive feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Welcome people with different experience levels and views.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or threats
- Trolling or repeated disruption
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sharing private information without permission
- Other behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment

## Enforcement

The project maintainer can edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

For repeated or serious violations, the maintainer can limit or end a person's
participation in the project.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ItisShikhar). For a private report, use a contact
method listed on the maintainer's profile. Do not share sensitive information
in public issues or pull requests.
